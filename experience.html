Auryn Callista Luftyansa

Hello, My name is Auryn Callista Luftyansa. I was born in Boyolali on October 8, 2009. I graduated from SD Muhammadiyah PK Boyolali, SMP N 1 Ampel, and SMK N Tengaran. These schools helped me become a disciplined person with a high curiosity for technology. Currently, I live in Rekuning, Banyuanyar, Ampel, Boyolali.

As a Web Developer and UI/UX Designer, I have worked on several important projects. One of my first projects was "Pikirkan Sebelum Membuang," which focuses on environmental awareness. I also created a "Sistem Manajemen" (Management System) to practice my skills in programming logic and software development.

I believe that technology can help people, so I developed a project called "Edukasi Untuk Perubahan." This project provides educational information through a digital platform. Additionally, I worked on a project named "Sow Noil Lomin" to further explore my technical abilities in creating functional web designs.

One of my favorite creative projects is "Empowering Brands Through Creative Solutions." In this project, I used my UI/UX design skills to help brands look professional and reach more people. It taught me how to combine beautiful design with smart technology to solve real problems for businesses.

Today, I continue to learn about the latest technology to improve my skills. My goal is to become a competent software developer in the future. I am excited to take on more challenges and build useful digital products that can make a positive impact on the world.
